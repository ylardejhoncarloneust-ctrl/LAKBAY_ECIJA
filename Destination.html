ALOHA FALLS

Aloha Falls is located in barangay Puncan in Carranglan, Nueva Ecija. Surrounded by the green foliage and about 15 meters to 20 meters in height. Aside from being picnic grove, the site is also perfect for trekking and mountain climbing.

CALABASA RIVER

The river is known for its crystal-clear, cold water, surrounded by lush forests and hills, making it a popular spot for tourists and locals. Visitors can enjoy various activities, such as swimming, picnicking, and exploring the natural surroundings. Historically, it has been a vital source for irrigation and fishing for the local community. Today, it is recognized as a prime location for eco-tourism in the province. It is easy to find by searching for "Calabasa Picnic River" on Waze or Google Maps. There is an environmental fee of ₱10 and a cottage rental fee of ₱500.

CRYSTAL WAVE HOTEL AND RESORT

Crystal Waves can refer to a hotel and resort in Talavera, Nueva Ecija, which features a wave pool, adventure activities like ziplining and wall climbing, and an events center. Alternatively, it can describe a glass art piece with a design of flowing blue and white bands, meant to be soothing and elegant.

CAMP PARAISO

Camp Paraiso is a Bali-inspired resort in Bongabon, Nueva Ecija, known as the "Bali of Nueva Ecija" for its scenic and tranquil atmosphere. It features numerous Instagrammable spots like a love nest and waterfalls, a man-made pool with cold spring water, and offers activities such as mountain climbing, river trekking, swimming, and glamping. Accommodations range from cabins to villas, and the resort also provides options like boodle fights and karaoke.

FARM RIDGE

Farm Ridge is a scenic agri-tourism destination and resort located in Pantabangan, Nueva Ecija, Philippines, managed by Desmond Farm and Partners Inc. (DFPI). It is known for its breathtaking, panoramic views of the Sierra Madre mountain range and the Pantabangan Lake/Dam.

GEORGE POINT DINOSAUR THEME PARK

George Point Dinosaur Theme Park is a nature park in Pantabangan, Nueva Ecija, featuring a prehistoric-themed area with life-sized dinosaurs and a Safari Theme Park with exotic wildlife. It's located in the Sierra Madre Mountain Ranges and offers other attractions like a White Sand Lagoon pool, a Playhouse, 4x4 rides, and views of Pantabangan Lake and a windmill. The park has a Dinosaur World with life-sized dinosaurs and a Safari Theme Park for encountering wildlife. The park offers cottages and gazebos for visitors, and free shuttle service to viewing spots.

MINALUNGAO NATIONAL PARK

Minalungao National Park is a protected area in General Tinio, Nueva Ecija, Philippines, known for its limestone formations and the Peñaranda River. It's promoted as an ecotourism destination with activities like raft riding, cliff diving, swimming, and hiking to the 1000-step hill to see a large glass cross. The park features up to 16-meter high limestone walls, unique rock formations, and a system of subterranean caverns.
