Tessie Lagalag

Sobrang ganda ng lugar! Perfect for relaxation — tahimik, malamig ang hangin, at ang ganda ng view sa villa. Worth it talaga yung stay namin dito sa Highland Bali Villas. Tapos ang linis din ng tubig sa Aloha Falls! Ang saya ng trek, hindi sobrang hirap pero sulit sa ganda ng falls. Plus, ang picnic setup solid sa sarap mga food packages.

Dodong Tikwas

Ang sarap ng food! Lalo na yung picnic lunch sa falls, sobrang unique experience. Busog na, fresh pa yung view. Maganda din yung villa, malinis, cozy, at may private view deck pa! Parang Bali vibes talaga kahit nasa Pilipinas lang.

Babylyn Takbuhan

The infinity pool view is amazing! Lalo na pag sunset — sobrang aesthetic. Balik kami for sure!

Bebang Walang Kupas

Tahimik, nature-filled, at relaxing — parang nag-slow down ang oras. Ideal place para mag-unwind at mag rechargeat maramdaman ang presensiya ng kalikasan. Parang isang lugar na ginawa para magpahinga ang isip at kaluluwa.

Mang Benjie

Ang saya ng buong trip! Walang dull moment, from falls trek to river swim. Perfect weekend escape!

Food Package

Two breakfasts (Day 2 & Day 3) at the resort’s restaurant — likely including continental breakfast items plus hot dishes (eggs, rice or bread, fresh fruit, coffee/tea) in an Asian-fusion setting.

One picnic-style lunch (Day 2) at the falls location — packed lunch format: rice or bread, local main dish (grilled or adobo-style), salad or vegetable side, bottled water or soft drink.

Two dinners (Day 1 & Day 2) at the resort’s dining venue — menus feature Asian-fusion cuisine: e.g., grilled fish or meat, vegetable sides, rice or pasta, dessert and non-alcoholic drinks.

Dining venue also offers international buffet on weekends and à la carte menus weekdays, so if one of the dinners happens on a weekend the buffet might apply.

Meals included do not (usually) cover specialty drinks, alcoholic beverages, extra snacks outside meal times, or third-party food outside the resort/picnic setting.

The resort accepts advance request for dietary preferences/allergies (worth confirming).
