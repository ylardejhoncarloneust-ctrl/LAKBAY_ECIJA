FARM RIDGE

Farm Ridge is a scenic agri-tourism destination and resort located in Pantabangan, Nueva Ecija, Philippines, managed by Desmond Farm and Partners Inc. (DFPI). It is known for its breathtaking, panoramic views of the Sierra Madre mountain range and the Pantabangan Lake/Dam.

Ramon Lagman

Grabe, sobrang ganda sa Farm Ridge! Para kang nasa gitna ng paraiso — puro bundok, hangin, at kalikasan ang makikita mo. The view is breathtaking, especially sa umaga kapag may fog pa at tila lumulutang ka sa ulap. 🌤️ Ang sarap mag-kape habang nakatingin sa mga taniman at sa buong valley. Ang mga cottages sobrang linis at cozy, at ‘yung staff very friendly at accommodating. Perfect place talaga kung gusto mong magpahinga at mag-reconnect sa nature. Parang ang layo mo sa gulo ng mundo!

Francisco Tumba

Farm Ridge is the perfect escape from city life! Ang ganda ng location — nasa mataas na bahagi kaya ang lamig ng hangin at sobrang presko. The scenery is stunning, especially during sunrise and sunset. 🌅 Makikita mo ‘yung mga bundok, taniman, at mga hayop sa paligid na parang nasa postcard. The cabins are rustic yet comfortable, tapos may mga activities pa like farming, animal feeding, at hiking. Ang saya kasi parang simple living pero with style! Highly recommended for anyone who wants peace, quiet, and beautiful views.

Teresa Hilig

Napaka-ganda talaga ng Farm Ridge! The whole place feels so calm and refreshing. Ang linis ng paligid, ang ganda ng landscaping, at ang view ng mountains — nakakawala talaga ng stress. 😍 You can just sit outside your cabin, breathe the fresh air, and enjoy the sound of nature. Perfect ito for couples, families, or kahit solo travelers na gusto lang mag-relax. Ang sarap sa pakiramdam kasi ang simple ng buhay doon, pero ang yaman sa tanawin at karanasan. Isa ‘to sa mga lugar na babalikan ko talaga!

Ricardo Bato

The view of the mountains and rice fields is just amazing — parang painting sa totoong buhay! 🎨 The air is fresh, the food is delicious, and the staff treat you like family. May mga bonfire at stargazing activities pa sa gabi, which makes it even more magical. Ang sarap magpahinga dito, lalo na kung gusto mong mag-detox sa social media at stress. Totally worth the trip!
