LAKE FARM DE LA MARRE

Lake Farm De La Marre Agri-tourism Park (Lake Farm De La Marre) is located at KM5 Brgy. Villarica, Pantatabangan, Nueva Ecija with a total land area of 25,501 square meters. It was originally intended for private use or retirement farmhouse but because of its location facing the Pantabangan Lake and the lush forests’, visitors’ arrival boomed. We started developing parts of the land by August 2021 to open to visitors and gather a large market by offering day tour activities like sightseeing, picnic, ATV rides, and overnight accommodations thru camping and Glamping (camping with style).
