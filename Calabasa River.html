CALABASA RIVER

The river is known for its crystal-clear, cold water, surrounded by lush forests and hills, making it a popular spot for tourists and locals. Visitors can enjoy various activities, such as swimming, picnicking, and exploring the natural surroundings. Historically, it has been a vital source for irrigation and fishing for the local community. Today, it is recognized as a prime location for eco-tourism in the province. It is easy to find by searching for "Calabasa Picnic River" on Waze or Google Maps. There is an environmental fee of ₱10 and a cottage rental fee of ₱500.
